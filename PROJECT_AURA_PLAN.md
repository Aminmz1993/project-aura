PROJECT AURA

Your Music. Your Mood. Your Sound.

Project Type: AI-Powered Cross-Platform Music Player
Platforms: Android and iOS
Initial Market: Iran
Target Market: Global

1. Product Vision

PROJECT AURA is an intelligent music player that understands music, adapts sound to the listening experience, and creates dynamic visual atmospheres.

Our goal is to build a premium-quality music experience that works directly on smartphones.

2. Core Features

AI Music Intelligence

- Analyze local music.
- Estimate mood, energy, tempo, and musical characteristics.
- Learn user preferences.

AI Smart DJ

- Intelligent music recommendations.
- Smart Shuffle.
- Mood Journey.
- Smooth transitions between compatible tracks.

AI Sound Engine

- Equalizer.
- Bass, midrange, and treble adjustment.
- Audio profiles for headphones and speakers.
- Loudness and clipping protection.

AI Live Atmosphere

- Dynamic visual effects.
- Music-reactive colors and animations.
- Mood-based visual themes.
- Immersive playback experience.

3. Platforms

- Android: Flutter UI with native Kotlin audio components.
- iOS: Flutter UI with native Swift audio components.
- English as the default language.
- Persian language support.
- Right-to-left and left-to-right layouts.

4. Technology

- Flutter
- Kotlin
- Swift
- Android Media3
- Apple AVFoundation
- SQLite
- On-device machine learning

Technology choices must be validated through real-device testing.

5. Privacy

- Local music analysis should happen on-device whenever possible.
- Personal listening data must be protected.
- Cloud processing requires user consent.
- No unauthorized access to music services or DRM-protected content.

6. Business Model

Freemium:

- Free basic music playback.
- Basic music analysis.
- Basic visualizer and equalizer.
- Premium AI DJ.
- Advanced sound optimization.
- Premium visual experiences.

7. MVP Development Priorities

1. Project foundation.
2. Professional user interface.
3. Local music library.
4. Reliable audio playback.
5. Now Playing screen.
6. Basic equalizer.
7. Music-reactive visualizer.
8. Initial AI music analysis.
9. Smart Shuffle.

8. Design Direction

- Dark futuristic interface.
- Neon purple, blue, and pink accents.
- Glassmorphism.
- Smooth animations.
- Premium visual identity.
- Responsive mobile-first design.

9. Development Principle

Build PROJECT AURA step by step, test on real Android and iOS devices, prioritize stability and audio quality, and avoid promising features that platform limitations do not support.
